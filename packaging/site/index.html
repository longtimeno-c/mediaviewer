MediaViewer

A fast photo and video viewer for Windows and macOS. Find any photo, or any moment in any video, by typing what you remember.

Free and open source. Search is computed on your own computer, and nothing about your files leaves it. What it does · Download

MediaViewer for NAS, on iPhone and iPad

Search the library MediaViewer indexed on your computer from your phone, anywhere, with the photos and clips coming from your own Synology NAS. Needs MediaViewer on a Mac or Windows PC and a Synology running the MediaViewer service.
