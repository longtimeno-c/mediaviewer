## Cut Video (Tella)

Pause table by tone, the conservative filler list, the false-start and retake rules (drop the earlier
attempt of a restatement, keep the last complete take, show every group of takes), never cutting
laughs, reactions or the beat before a punchline.

## video-use (Browser Use)

Silences of 400 ms or more as the cleanest cuts, air at speaker handoffs, propose a short strategy and
wait for a yes, checking every cut boundary of the result and stopping after three passes.
